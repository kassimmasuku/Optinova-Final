# Optinova Vision Hub

Create a modern, professional, mobile-responsive website for an eye care clinic called Optinova Eye Care, located in Harare, Zimbabwe.

The website must have a clean medical design, smooth animations, fast loading speed, and SEO optimization.

The overall style should feel professional, trustworthy, and welcoming, similar to a modern private medical clinic website.

Color palette:

Primary: Deep Blue
Secondary: White
Accent: Soft Green / Teal

Important Visual Requirement

All images used throughout the website should include diverse ethnic representation, especially Black African patients and professionals, while also including other ethnic groups.

Examples of images to use:

Black optometrist performing an eye exam
Black family choosing glasses
Multicultural patients receiving eye tests
Diverse children during vision testing
Patients selecting eyeglass frames

The imagery must reflect Zimbabwe's diverse community and feel relatable to local patients.

Eye Health & Vision Facts Page

Create an interactive educational section with clickable fact cards.

Each card should expand into a detailed explanation.

Fact 1

Title

Why Eye Exams Are Important

Expanded Information

Regular eye examinations help detect conditions such as glaucoma, cataracts, and diabetic eye disease before symptoms appear. Early detection allows for better treatment and protection of vision.

Eye specialists recommend having an eye examination every one to two years.

Fact 2

Title

Digital Eye Strain

Expanded Information

Long hours spent using computers, smartphones, and tablets can cause digital eye strain.

Common symptoms include:

Dry eyes
Headaches
Blurred vision
Eye fatigue

Taking regular breaks and using blue light protection lenses can help reduce discomfort.

Fact 3

Title

Children and Vision Health

Expanded Information

Vision problems in children can affect learning and development.

Early eye exams ensure children can see clearly in school and during daily activities.

Fact 4

Title

Glaucoma Awareness

Expanded Information

Glaucoma damages the optic nerve and can lead to permanent vision loss if untreated.

Regular eye pressure tests help detect glaucoma early, even before symptoms appear.
